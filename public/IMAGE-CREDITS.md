# Stock photography — provenance and swap list

Every file here is a PLACEHOLDER from Unsplash, chosen to hold the right
composition until a real IES site photograph replaces it. The Unsplash licence
allows commercial use without attribution, but the reason to replace these is
not licensing — it is that this page's entire argument is that everything on it
is checkable, and a stock photograph of somebody else's job is the one element
that cannot be.

Replace in this order. The first three carry the most weight.

### Two rules these placeholders now follow

**No other firm's branding.** Several otherwise good candidates were rejected
because the worker wore a branded uniform or stood beside a branded van. Putting
another contractor's logo on this page is worse than a weak photograph.
Equipment branding (the Hager breakers in `electrician.jpg`) is different and
unavoidable — it is what a UK sparks actually installs.

**No geography tell.** The two shots replaced here were an American asphalt
shingle roof and an American-pattern meter job with no hi-vis. Continental
candidates were also rejected: terracotta pantiles and cypress trees read
Mediterranean, not West Yorkshire.

### Known weakness

The set is now board-heavy — `consumer-unit.jpg`, `testing.jpg`,
`panel-wiring.jpg` and `electrician.jpg` are all close-ups of distribution
boards, differentiated only by crop and colour cast. Stock cannot fix that.
Four photographs of your own work, on four different jobs, would.
